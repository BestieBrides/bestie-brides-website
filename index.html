Our Story

It Started With Two Best Friends.

Our story begins with two best friends whom God brought together to help one another navigate the struggles of life.

From personal life challenges to seasons when simply being able to eat or pay rent felt difficult, we learned firsthand what it means to need someone in your corner.

Then it hit us: every bride deserves that same kind of support.

We saw the stress levels that brides endure during their wedding journey. What is supposed to be a season full of beautiful memories can quickly turn into chaos.

Just like God put us together to help one another through stressful seasons, we believe every bride deserves a Bestie by her side through the good times and the tough times of her wedding journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Bestie Brides wedding planners?

Bestie Brides is designed specifically around the bride. Our role is bride-focused support. We help organize information, reminders, appointments, decisions and communication so the bride has dedicated support throughout her journey.

Who is Bestie Brides for?

Our ideal bride is someone who wants professional support by her side during the wedding journey, especially when she does not have someone who can provide that kind of dedicated help.

What does the $7,000 starting package include?

The Bestie Brides Package is designed to provide comprehensive bride-centered support. Your consultation will help determine the exact scope and priorities for your wedding journey.

Can I customize my Bestie experience?

Yes. Add-ons are available at $500 each, and custom support can be discussed during your consultation.

When should I book my Bestie?

Earlier is generally better because it gives your Bestie more time to help organize your journey. Your consultation will determine whether Bestie Brides is the right fit for your wedding timeline.
